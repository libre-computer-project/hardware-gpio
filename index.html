Libre Computer GPIO Pinout

Each pin's square is banded: the top is the board's colour for that pin (yellow I2C, blue SPI, orange 3.3V), and below it one vertical stripe per other mux the pad can reach. A GPIO-only pin has stripes alone. Select a pin for its full pinmux, again to close, or select a legend entry to filter.
